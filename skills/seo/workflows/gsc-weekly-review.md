# Search Console Weekly Review

A lightweight weekly SEO review once Search Console data exists.

## Goal

Catch:
- emerging traffic opportunities
- CTR wins
- new indexing problems
- page/template regressions

## Weekly cadence

## 1. Performance overview

Check the last 28 days vs previous 28 days for:
- clicks
- impressions
- average CTR
- average position

Then segment by:
- pages
- queries
- device
- country (if relevant)

## 2. Find CTR opportunities

Look for queries/pages with:
- high impressions
- average position roughly 1-10
- below-expected CTR

Then run the `ctr-optimization.md` workflow.

## 3. Check emerging winners

Look for pages/queries with impression growth but mediocre positions.

These pages often need:
- better internal linking
- fresher titles/descriptions
- content expansion
- stronger schema or E-E-A-T signals

## 4. Check indexing health

Review:
- submitted vs indexed gaps
- sudden increases in duplicate/canonical issues
- Crawled - currently not indexed patterns
- new pages not appearing as expected

## 5. Check Core Web Vitals

Look for:
- new regressions by template/device
- URLs falling from Good to Needs Improvement/Poor
- pattern clusters, not just one-off URLs

## 6. Review recent launches

For pages launched in the last 2-6 weeks, verify:
- they were added to sitemap
- they are internally linked
- they are getting impressions
- titles/meta/canonicals look correct

## 7. Summarize actions

Produce 3 buckets:
- quick wins this week
- investigations to queue
- watchlist items

## Good weekly review behavior

- look for patterns, not isolated noise
- prioritize pages that matter commercially
- avoid changing titles/meta on everything at once
- keep a simple changelog of what was changed and when
